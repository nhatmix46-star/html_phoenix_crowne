PHOENIX CROWNE VINH HOTEL

Genuine luxury is defined by serenity

In the vibrant heart of Vinh City — the economic, cultural, and commercial hub of the North Central region — Phoenix Crowne Vinh Hotel rises as a new symbol of elegance, refinement, and international five-star distinction.

Ideally located at 78 Nguyen Thi Minh Khai, one of the city’s most prestigious central boulevards, the hotel is envisioned as a dynamic meeting point for business leaders, discerning travelers, and the local community. Here, every stay goes beyond accommodation, transforming into a thoughtfully curated, deeply personalized, and truly memorable experience.
